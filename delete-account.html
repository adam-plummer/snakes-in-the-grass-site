Delete Your Account

Snakes in the Grass · Adam Plummer

You can delete your Snakes in the Grass account, and the data that goes with it, at any time. There are two ways.

In the game (straight away)

  1. Open the game and tap Profile on the home screen.
  2. On the Record page, tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then Sure? Delete everything to confirm.

Your phone needs an internet connection. Everything listed below is deleted there and then, and the game starts again as a new install.

By email (if you no longer have the game)

Email snakesinthegrass.game@outlook.com with the subject Delete my account and your player name from the leaderboard (for example Muddy Badger), so I can find your records. I will delete them and reply within one month.

What is deleted

  • your scores on every leaderboard, and your player name;
  • your public profile record;
  • the link to your Google Play Games account, and the private backup of your farm;
  • your play data held by Unity Analytics, if you shared it;
  • in the game route, your farm and saves on that phone.

What is kept

  • Purchases. Remove Ads and the characters belong to your Google account, and Google Play keeps its own record of them, so they come back if you install the game again.
  • Crash reports. These carry no name or player ID and are deleted automatically after Unity's retention period.

For everything the game collects and why, see the privacy policy.
