Testers

Snakes in the Grass · closed test, Android only

Thanks for helping! Snakes in the Grass is a day-and-night farming game. By day you mow, plant and sell. By night, snakes come out of whatever grass you left uncut, and you keep them off your crops, animals and house with a torch, a shovel, traps and lures.

Before a new developer can release a game, Google needs at least 12 people to test it for 14 days. That's what you're joining.

Getting it

  1. Join the testers' group. That's how Google Play knows you're a tester.
    Join the group
  2. Opt in to the test, using the same Google account.
    Opt in
  3. Install the game from the Google Play link on the opt-in page. It can take a few minutes after you opt in before Play lets you install it.

It's Android only for now, so iPhones can't join, sorry.

The one big ask: please keep the game installed and stay opted in for the full 14 days, even if you only play a few times. If too many people drop out, the 14 days start again.

What to do

Play however you like. If you can, play your first day without reading up on anything first. Seeing how a brand-new player gets on is exactly what I can't test myself.

A day takes about 4 minutes. A season is 15 days, about an hour in total, and you can stop and carry on whenever you like.

When the game asks about PLAY DATA, please say Yes. It sends me anonymous numbers about each night, such as how far you got and what was lost, which is how I see where people get stuck. It's never linked to your name, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Good to know

  • Updates come through the Play Store like any app, so there's no need to uninstall. If you're signed in to Google Play Games, your homestead, record and leaderboard name come back after a reinstall or on a new phone. A season you're partway through stays on the phone it started on.
  • The ads are real. Because the game isn't in the shop yet, they may not always turn up; if an ad offer doesn't appear, that's why. Please only watch one when you want the reward. Watching or tapping ads to help me out can get my ad account closed.
  • You don't need to buy anything. Any purchase would be real money, so please don't.
  • Your best season goes on the public leaderboard under a name you pick from a list. It's never your real name.

Feedback

Please fill in the short form twice: once after your first night, and once near the end of the two weeks. It takes about three minutes.

Give feedback

One honest line beats a polite paragraph. "I didn't see the snake until it bit me" is gold.

Questions or problems? Email snakesinthegrass.game@outlook.com. See the privacy policy for everything the game collects.
