WHAT IT IS

Grandad left you the farm, and the grass. Every patch you don't mow by dusk is
somewhere a snake can hide.

By day you farm: mow the long grass, sow barley, wheat and maize, keep chickens,
sheep and cows, and sell what you grow. Every second counts, because the day is
your real budget.

By night the snakes come out of whatever you left uncut. Light them up with your
torch, whack them with your shovel, set traps and lures, and keep them off your
crops, your animals and your house.

Fifteen days make a season, about an hour of play, resumable between any day.
Lose the house or run out of money and the season ends, but nothing is wasted:
every season pays heirlooms to spend on the homestead, so the next one starts
stronger.


WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

Mowing is the strategy. In most farming games cutting grass is chores; here it
is the decision the night is built on. Snakes come up out of the grass you left
standing, so where you mow by day is where you are safe after dark, and each day
is a fresh puzzle rather than a routine.


FEATURES

- A day-and-night farming game played in short, tense 15-day seasons
- 14 homestead unlocks to earn across seasons
- 10 skulls to make a season harder for a bigger payout
- Weekly and all-time leaderboards
- 39 Google Play Games achievements
- Seven farmers to play as, from Tom and Rosie to a scarecrow and a snowman
- Share a picture of your best farm
- Sign in with Play Games to keep your farm on a new phone
- Plays offline; no account needed


HISTORY

Made single-handed in Unity over 2026, from a design document to a closed test
with 22 testers in October 2026, and a soft launch the same autumn. The art is
modelled, rigged and textured for the game; the look is a British smallholding
in parchment and ink.
