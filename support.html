Support

Snakes in the Grass

Found a bug, lost a save, or stuck on something? Email snakesinthegrass.game@outlook.com and I'll get back to you, usually within a few days.

Reporting a problem

It helps to include:

  • what happened, and what you were doing just before;
  • your phone's make and model;
  • the game's version (shown on its Google Play page, under "About this game");
  • your player name from the leaderboard, if the problem is with your scores or profile.

Deleting your data

To have your leaderboard scores, public profile and play data deleted, email snakesinthegrass.game@outlook.com with your player name from the leaderboard. Your saves are only on your phone; uninstalling the game deletes them. See the privacy policy for everything the game collects.
